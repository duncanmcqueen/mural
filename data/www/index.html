Re-home belts

1. Place the belt loops on the homing screws and retract each belt until its motor stalls. Don't let the motors stall for too long.

2. Extend to the home position. Make sure the belts are unobstructed and the motors don't skip, or drawing accuracy will suffer.

Pen calibration

Insert the pen so it's close to, but not touching the wall and secure it with a bolt. Then set the down angle so the pen just touches the wall, and the up angle so it clears the wall without the servo straining against the end of its slot.
