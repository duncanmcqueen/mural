Hobby servos give no feedback: these are the commanded values, not the measured horn position. If the horn is visibly not where it's told to be, or it buzzes/shudders while holding, it's probably straining against the slot or binding.

Servo settings

Limits are hard stops in firmware: nothing (calibration, drawing, tests) can command the servo outside them. Set them just inside the range where the horn moves freely. Slower speed = less current spike per move.

System

Adds a "Starting the move to x, y" line per drawing command. Off by default: each line holds up the motors for about 35 ms (serial runs at 9600 baud), which slows drawing, and it quickly pushes older entries out of the log.
